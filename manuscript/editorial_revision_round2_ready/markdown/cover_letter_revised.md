# Cover Letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider the revised original research manuscript, "Suppression-Aware County-Level Epilepsy and Status Epilepticus Mortality in the United States, 2019–2024," for publication in Epilepsy & Behavior.

This manuscript presents a national ecological county-level analysis of epilepsy/status epilepticus-related mortality using public aggregate CDC WONDER data. The revised package addresses a full pre-submission editorial-methodological review. Major revisions include reframing the suppression scenario hierarchy around reconciliation to the known county total, adding a constant-mean stress test, embedding populated main Tables 1-5, replacing the temporal/COVID figure so it covers 2019-2024, and expanding the comparison with prior censored-WONDER methods.

The work is not under consideration elsewhere and has not been published previously. The author approves submission. No external funding was received, and the author declares no competing interests. The study used public, aggregate, deidentified data and was deemed not human-subjects research.
